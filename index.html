AMAZON ADS ANALYZER · CHROME EXTENSION

Privacy Policy

Transparency about how this extension handles your data.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Overview

Amazon Ads Analyzer (the "Extension") helps users analyze advertising-related information displayed on Amazon pages and organize results locally. This Privacy Policy explains how the Extension handles information and how its account authentication service works.

2. Advertising and Page Data

Advertising and product information extracted or analyzed by the Extension is processed and stored locally on the user's device. This data is not uploaded to Supabase by the Extension. Users may choose to export their results to local files.

3. Account and Authentication Data

The Extension uses Supabase solely to provide account sign-in, authentication, and access authorization. Depending on the authentication method and implementation, this may involve an email address, user identifier, authentication tokens, and authorization status. These details are processed as needed to authenticate users and manage access to the Extension.

4. Purpose and Sharing

Account-related information is used for authentication and access management, not for advertising profiling or selling personal information. Supabase acts as the service provider for these functions. The Extension does not send locally analyzed Amazon advertising data to Supabase.

5. Storage and Security

Advertising analysis results remain on the user's device unless the user independently exports or shares them. Authentication and authorization information may be processed or stored through Supabase. We aim to limit access to account information to what is necessary for operating the service. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention and Deletion

Users can remove locally stored extension data by clearing the Extension's data or uninstalling it, subject to browser behavior and any files already exported. For requests to access or delete account-related data stored through Supabase, contact us using the email below. We will review and handle requests as applicable.

7. Third-Party Services

Supabase provides authentication and authorization infrastructure. For details about Supabase's data practices, see Supabase Privacy Policy . Amazon and Google Chrome are third-party platforms and have their own policies. Amazon Ads Analyzer is an independent extension and is not represented here as being endorsed by Amazon.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Extension's features or data practices change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

1. 概述

Amazon Ads Analyzer（以下简称“本插件”） 帮助用户分析亚马逊网页中展示的广告相关信息， 并在本地整理分析结果。 本隐私政策说明本插件如何处理用户信息， 以及账号登录验证服务的运作方式。

2. 广告及网页数据

本插件提取或分析的广告及商品信息， 仅在用户设备本地处理和保存。 本插件不会将这些广告分析数据上传至 Supabase。 用户可以自主将分析结果导出为本地文件。

3. 账号及身份验证数据

本插件仅使用 Supabase 提供账号登录、 身份验证和使用权限管理。 根据实际登录方式及技术实现， 相关信息可能包括邮箱地址、用户标识、 身份验证令牌和授权状态。 这些信息仅用于验证用户身份及管理插件使用权限。

4. 使用目的与共享

账号相关信息仅用于登录验证和授权管理， 不用于广告画像或出售个人信息。 Supabase 作为上述功能的服务提供商。 本插件不会向 Supabase 传输本地分析的 亚马逊广告数据。

5. 存储与安全

广告分析结果保留在用户设备上， 除非用户自行导出或分享。 身份验证和授权信息可能通过 Supabase 处理或保存。 我们致力于将账号信息的访问限制在 服务运行所必需的范围内。 但任何在线服务都无法保证绝对安全。

6. 保存与删除

用户可通过清除插件数据或卸载插件 删除本地保存的数据， 具体受浏览器行为影响。 已经导出的文件需由用户自行管理。 如需查询或删除通过 Supabase 保存的 账号相关数据，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。 我们将根据适用要求处理相关请求。
